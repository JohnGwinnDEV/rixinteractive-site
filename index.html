Meet J. C. Rix

Author. Technology architect. World builder.

J. C. Rix writes stories where science, mythology, history, and imagination collide. His work is shaped by a career designing complex systems and a lifelong interest in understanding how people, technologies, and civilizations respond to extraordinary change.
